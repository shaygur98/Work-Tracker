Work Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the app stores

Work Time stores the shifts you log (date, start and end time) and your settings (hourly rate, theme, accent color) in your browser on your own device. This data is never sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Connect Google Drive", the app asks Google for permission to use a hidden app-specific folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). The app uses this permission only to save and read a single backup file of your shifts and settings. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

Your backup goes directly from your browser to your own Google Drive. The developer has no access to it. The app does not read your name, email address or any other Google account information.

You can disconnect at any time from the app. To delete the backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Work Time → Delete hidden app data.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no analytics, ads or trackers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Work Time's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
